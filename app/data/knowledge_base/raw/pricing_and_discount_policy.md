# Pricing and Discount Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This synthetic policy defines the approval controls for discounts and non-standard commercial terms on NexusFlow software subscriptions. It is intended for internal sales management, Finance, and Revenue Operations use.

This training document does not contain a production price book. It does not establish a customer-specific price, quote, or contractual entitlement. The latest approved quote and price book remain the authoritative sources for an actual transaction.

## 2. Discount calculation

For this policy, the discount percentage is calculated against the approved list price for the same product, quantity, term, and billing basis:

**Discount percentage = (approved list amount minus proposed net recurring amount) divided by approved list amount, multiplied by 100.**

Taxes, separately priced implementation services, and approved pass-through expenses must not be included in the recurring subscription discount calculation unless the quote policy explicitly says otherwise.

A discount must be evaluated against the approved price book that applies to the quote. An AI assistant must not invent a list price, infer a discount from a net amount alone, or convert currencies without an approved exchange-rate source.

## 3. Discount approval bands

The following approval bands apply to the synthetic training policy:

| Discount from approved list price | Required approver |
|---|---|
| Up to and including 10% | Sales representative may request and apply within their delegated authority, subject to standard quote controls. |
| Greater than 10% and up to and including 20% | Sales manager approval is required before the quote is sent. |
| Greater than 20% and up to and including 30% | Sales leader approval is required before the quote is sent. |
| Greater than 30% | Chief Revenue Officer and Finance approval are both required before the quote is sent. |

An approver may reject a discount request even when it falls within their approval band. Approval authority does not require an approver to approve a request.

## 4. Non-standard commercial terms

The following require Finance and Legal review regardless of discount percentage:

- Payment terms outside the standard approved terms.
- Contract terms that create uncapped liability or unusual indemnity obligations.
- Custom service-level commitments not covered by the standard service description.
- Unusual termination, refund, or price-protection provisions.
- Bespoke commitments that materially change the commercial risk of the deal.

A multi-year commitment does not automatically authorize an additional discount. Any stacking of term-based and discretionary discounts must be explicitly approved and recorded.

## 5. Approval evidence and quote controls

The opportunity or deal record must contain:

- The applicable approved price-book reference and version.
- Product, quantity, term, billing basis, and currency.
- List amount and proposed net amount.
- Calculated discount percentage.
- Name or role of each required approver.
- Approval date and written approval evidence.
- Any non-standard terms and the required Finance or Legal decision.

A quote must not be represented as approved until all required approvals have been recorded. Verbal indication of support is not a substitute for approval evidence.

## 6. Renewals and expansions

Renewal and expansion quotes must use the applicable approved price book and renewal rules. A prior customer discount does not automatically carry forward to a renewal or expansion. Any exception requires the approval specified in this policy.

## 7. Questions the policy cannot answer

This document intentionally does not provide product list prices, customer-specific negotiated prices, tax treatment, or current exchange rates. Those values must be retrieved from the approved price book, quote, contract, or Finance source. If the relevant source is unavailable, the answer must say so rather than estimate a price.
